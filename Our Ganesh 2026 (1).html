🗓️ Puja Schedule & Rituals

14 September 2026 (Ganesh Chaturthi)

07:30 AM • 14 Sept
ମୂର୍ତ୍ତି ସ୍ଥାପନା ଓ ପ୍ରାଣ ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା (Pran Pratishtha)
Sacred welcome of Lord Ganesha to our mandap with Vedic chants & kalash sthapana.
10:30 AM • 14 Sept
ମହା ପୁଷ୍ପାଞ୍ଜଳି ଓ ଯଜ୍ଞ (Pushpanjali & Havan)
Special flower offering ceremony for all families and distant brothers.
01:00 PM • 14 Sept
ମହା ପ୍ରସାଦ ସେବନ (Maha Prasad Distribution)
Community feast (Anna Prasad, Dalma, Kheeri & Khaja) for all villagers and guests.
07:00 PM • 14 Sept
ସନ୍ଧ୍ୟା ଆଳତି ଓ ସାଂସ୍କୃତିକ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ (Sandhya Aarti & Bhajan)
108 Diya Deepa Aarti followed by Bhajan Sandhya, dance performances by kids, and prize distributions.
Next Day • 15 Sept
ବିସର୍ଜନ ଶୋଭାଯାତ୍ରା (Visarjan Shobhayatra)
Grand procession with traditional dhols, dulduli, sankirtan, and colors through our village streets.
